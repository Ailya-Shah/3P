Three letters. Three disciplines. One notebook.

I kept noticing that the things I care about don't sit in separate boxes. A
physics problem is really a question about which model to trust. A machine
learning model is really a question about people — who it helps, who it misses,
what it quietly assumes. And Python is the thread that lets me actually *try*
any of it instead of just thinking about it.

So this site is named after the three lenses I keep reaching for.

## Physics

The habit of reasoning from first principles, checking units, and asking whether
a result is even the right *order of magnitude* before celebrating it. Physics is
where I learned that a clean answer to the wrong question is still wrong.

## Psyche

Most of my projects end up touching the human side of things — therapy outcomes,
sleep, how people learn a language. Psychology is the reminder that data
describes people, and that "accuracy" is never the only thing worth measuring.

## Python

The part where ideas become runnable. pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Streamlit —
the tools I use to turn a hunch into something you can open, poke at, and argue
with.

## What goes here

Expect project write-ups, learning logs, short technical notes, and the
occasional essay that tries to connect two of these worlds. Some posts will be
careful and finished; others will be me thinking out loud. That's the point of a
notebook.

If you want the short version of everything below: I like turning data into
stories, code into useful systems, and ideas into things people can inspect.
