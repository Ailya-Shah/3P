I'm a data science undergraduate at **NUST SEECS**, focused on analysis, machine
learning, and building projects that make abstract ideas concrete.

My background includes programming fundamentals, object-oriented programming,
data structures and algorithms, databases, Python, and SQL — alongside applied
work with pandas, NumPy, Streamlit, and end-to-end machine learning workflows.

## Why "3P"

This site is organised around three lenses I keep coming back to:

- **Physics** — systems, models, and the discipline of reasoning from first principles.
- **Psyche** — psychology and the human side of the problems data is meant to serve.
- **Python** — the tooling that turns all of it into something you can run and inspect.

It's intentionally closer to a personal publication than a static CV. I use it to
share project notes, technical write-ups, and the ideas that shape how I work.

The quick version of what I do: I like turning data into stories, code into useful
systems, and ideas into projects that people can inspect.
